what is it?

What is collaboration?

Collaboration is when people work together for one goal. Everyone is good at something different, so together you can do more than alone.

It is not only group projects. It is also listening to others, sharing your ideas and helping when someone needs it.

Collaboration is when people work together for one goal. It's very important because you can't do everything alone. At school we have group projects, we share ideas and help each other. In the future every job needs teamwork, so it's a useful skill.

why is it important?

Why collaboration is important

We are 15-18 years old. Soon we go to university and start working, and there you need to work with other people all the time.

🤝

More trust

When you work together, you start to trust each other. This is how good teams and friendships start.

💡

More ideas

Every person thinks in a different way. When you put ideas together, you get better and more creative ones.

🚀

Less stress

When you split a big task, it's not so hard. You don't have to do everything alone.

🗣️

Better communication

You learn how to explain your ideas, listen to others and agree on things.

🏢

Needed for jobs

The biggest companies in the world work in teams. Almost every job interview asks about teamwork.

🎯

Reach goals faster

When everyone works for one goal, the team finishes faster.

where do we use it?

At school, at home, at work

  • Group projects, like this one. Everyone does the part they are good at.
  • Studying with friends before exams and explaining topics to each other.
  • Sports, school events and clubs. They don't work without teamwork.
  • Planning a trip or a party with friends.
  • Helping at home, for example cooking or cleaning together.
  • Solving arguments by listening to each other.
  • In IT companies programmers and designers make one product together.
  • In hospitals doctors and nurses work as one team.
  • Many big companies were started by two or three friends, not by one person.
a real example

How collaboration helps us at GoPrep

I work at Aleem, the company that makes GoPrep, an app that helps students prepare for exams. I am Head of Growth, so my job is to help the company grow and get more users. We also work with more than 500 creators who make ads about GoPrep on social media. That is collaboration too, just outside our team.

We split the tasks

Everyone has their own part of the work. Together all these parts become one app.

We discuss ideas

We talk about ideas as a team and choose the best one, not just the idea of one person.

We help each other

If someone is stuck, others help, so we don't lose time.

We work for one goal

Everyone knows what we are working for. That is how GoPrep got to more than 700,000 uses.

"In a team of 20+ people it's impossible to work without collaboration. You have to split the tasks, or nothing works."me, Head of Growth at GoPrep
